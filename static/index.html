03 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions.
Clear answers.

What does Sentinel moderate?

Sentinel monitors new Roblox group uploads and can archive audio, decals, videos, meshes, plugins, animations, models, and packages. System-generated images cannot be archived through Roblox's API.

Can I choose who is allowed to upload?

Yes. Add trusted uploader usernames or user IDs to your allowlist. You can use one allowlist for all asset types, or create specific lists for individual types.

How do I connect my Roblox account?

Sign in to Sentinel, then securely connect a dedicated moderation account from your dashboard. Use an account with permission to manage the groups you add. Its session credential stays in server memory and is not saved to the database.

Can I undo a moderation action?

You can restore archived assets from your moderation history when Roblox permits the action and your connected account has the required permissions. Restored assets are excluded from automatic re-archiving.

Does monitoring work when I close the page?

Monitoring runs on the Sentinel server, not in your browser. It continues while the server is running and your connected Roblox session remains valid. After a server restart, you will need to reconnect your moderation account.
